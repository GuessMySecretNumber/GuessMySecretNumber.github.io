Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Guess My Secret Number is a logic game with optional online and voice chat features.

Microphone

The app requests microphone access only when you choose to use voice chat during an online game. You can deny or disable microphone access in iOS Settings.

Online Play

Online features may process information required to operate a game session, such as room codes, player names, and gameplay state.

Support Communications

If you contact support by email, the information you include in your message is used to respond to your support request.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact guessmysecretnumber.support@gmail.com.
