Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Choose a game mode, set your secret number, and use the Bull, Cow, and Miss clues to discover your opponent's number before they discover yours.

Can I play against a BOT?

Yes. Select Play Against BOT and choose the difficulty level you want.

How do online rooms work?

Create an online room and share the room code or room link with your friend. Your friend can use it to join the match.

Why does the app request microphone access?

Microphone access is used only when you choose to use voice chat during an online game.

How do I enable the microphone after denying permission?

Open iPhone Settings, locate Secret Number, and enable microphone access if the option is available.
